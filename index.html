Common Questions

Where do you deliver?

Across Bengaluru, from the airport to Bannerghatta.

Same day. Bagaluru, Hennur, Thanisandra, Hegde Nagar, Yelahanka, Jakkur and Hebbal usually get delivery in 60 to 90 minutes.
