What should I do if my gas cylinder starts leaking?

Stay calm and act fast. Turn the regulator knob to OFF and switch off the stove. Do not switch any electrical switch on or off, and do not light a match, lighter or candle. Open all doors and windows, then move everyone outside and keep phones away from the cylinder until you are out. From outside, call the 24x7 LPG emergency helpline 1906. If you see fire or hear loud hissing, call 101 or 112 as well. Once you are safe, message or call us on +91 74832 22174. Do not try to repair the leak yourself.

Which gas brands do you supply?

Private gas brands such as Total Gas, Jyoti Gas and Agni Gas. Ask us what is in stock today.
